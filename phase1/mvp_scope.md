# MVP Scope (Phase 1 Locked)

## In Scope (MVP)
1. Authentication: Email/password, Apple/Google OAuth, guest mode.
2. Catalog browsing: genre/key/BPM/difficulty/tags filters + text search.
3. Playback core: play/pause/seek, background playback, lock-screen controls.
4. Guitar controls: tempo adjust, transpose, A-B looping, quick presets.
5. Library: favorites, recents, playlists (create/edit/reorder).
6. Monetization: free/premium gates + paywall + entitlement checks.
7. Reliability baseline: logs/metrics/alerts for playback/auth/catalog critical paths.
8. Rights-safe media delivery: signed playback URLs with pre-stream rights check.

## Explicit Non-Goals (MVP)
1. Community/social feed.
2. User-uploaded tracks.
3. DAW-like track editing.
4. Multi-stem mixer.
5. Desktop app.
6. Advanced ML personalization beyond rule-based recommendations.

## Scope Guardrails
- Any new feature not listed above defaults to **Out of Scope** until approved via change-control workflow.
- No untracked scope additions may enter active sprint execution.
